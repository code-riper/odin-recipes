Chicken Chow

Description

Chicken Chow is a popular Chinese-style dish made with stir-fried noodles, chicken, and vegetables. It is cooked with sauces like soy sauce, giving it a savory and slightly spicy taste. It is widely enjoyed as a fast food meal for its delicious flavor and quick preparation.

Ingredients

  • Chicken (boneless, thin slices)
  • Noodles (chowmein noodles)
  • Onion (sliced)
  • Cabbage (shredded)
  • Carrot (thin strips)
  • Capsicum (sliced)
  • Garlic (chopped)
  • 🧂 Sauces & Seasoning
  • Soy sauce
  • Vinegar
  • Chili sauce (optional)
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Boil noodles and keep aside
  2. Cook chicken in oil
  3. Add vegetables and stir fry
  4. Add sauces & seasoning
  5. Mix noodles with everything
  6. Cook for 2 minutes and serve
